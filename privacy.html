SwipeCalm for iPhone

Privacy Policy

SwipeCalm processes your photo library on your iPhone. Your photos are not uploaded to SwipeCalm servers.

Effective: July 10, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how the SwipeCalm iOS app and this website handle information. SwipeCalm does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your photos.

2. Photos and on-device analysis

When you grant access through Apple Photos, SwipeCalm reads the photos you select or permit so it can display them, organize your review queue, estimate storage, identify similar images, and perform Best Shot analysis. These operations run on your device.

SwipeCalm does not upload your photos, thumbnails, visual feature data, or analysis results to SwipeCalm servers. If you use iCloud Photos, iOS may download an image from your personal iCloud library to your device. Some iCloud-only items may be skipped when they are not locally available.

A left swipe does not immediately delete a photo. It adds a reference to SwipeCalm's in-app trash. Apple Photos permanently removes an item only after you explicitly confirm deletion and approve the system request.

3. Data stored on your device

SwipeCalm stores app state locally, including reviewed-photo identifiers, in-app trash entries, daily swipe counts, aggregate cleanup statistics, preferences, onboarding state, and temporary analysis cache data. This information supports undo, limits, progress, and restoring the app's interface.

Local app data remains on your device until it is removed by app controls, iOS, or deletion of the app. Photos remain governed by Apple Photos and your iCloud settings.

4. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes App Store purchases. SwipeCalm uses RevenueCat to determine Premium entitlement and restore purchases. For that purpose, RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription history, App Store receipt information, and technical details such as app version, device type, operating system, and IP address. SwipeCalm does not provide your photos to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat handles this information under its privacy policy. Apple handles purchase information under Apple's privacy policy.

5. Analytics, advertising, and the website

SwipeCalm does not include advertising networks or third-party product analytics SDKs. The public website does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account forms, or cookies set by SwipeCalm.

6. Sharing and sale

SwipeCalm does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide purchases and Premium entitlement, or when required by applicable law.

7. Security and your choices

You control photo access in iOS Settings and can limit the app to selected photos. Revoking access stops SwipeCalm from reading the affected library items. You can remove local app data by deleting the app. Purchase records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat according to their legal and retention obligations.

No method of storage or processing is completely secure, but SwipeCalm reduces exposure by keeping photo processing on your device and collecting no SwipeCalm account profile.

8. Children

SwipeCalm is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect account or profile information from children. Purchases are subject to Apple ID family and parental controls.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SwipeCalm's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact route on the SwipeCalm Support page. Do not include photos, purchase receipts, or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.
