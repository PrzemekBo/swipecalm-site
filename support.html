Troubleshooting

Photos are missing

Open iOS Settings, choose Apps, SwipeCalm, then Photos. Select Full Access to review the full library, or edit the selected-photo set. Return to SwipeCalm and refresh the screen.

A photo was swiped left by mistake

Use Undo before leaving the swipe flow, or open Trash and restore the item. SwipeCalm does not permanently delete a photo until you confirm deletion in Trash and approve the Apple Photos prompt.

Premium is not active

Open the Premium screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase. Check that the App Store can connect, then relaunch SwipeCalm if the entitlement is still pending.

Best Shot skipped iCloud photos

Best Shot runs on the iPhone. Keep the device online, open the affected photos in Apple Photos to download them, then run the scan again. SwipeCalm reports how many iCloud-only items could not be analyzed.

Deletion is not reflected immediately

Confirm the Apple Photos permission prompt and check Recently Deleted in Photos. iCloud Photos synchronization may take time across devices even after local deletion succeeds.
