Questions, answered

Does SwipeCalm delete a photo as soon as I swipe left?

No. A left swipe creates an item in SwipeCalm's in-app trash. The photo is permanently removed from your Apple Photos library only after you confirm deletion in the Trash screen.

Do my photos leave the device?

SwipeCalm does not upload photos to SwipeCalm servers. If you use iCloud Photos, iOS may download an original from your own iCloud library so it can be displayed or analyzed locally.

What is included for free?

The free tier includes 25 swipes per day. Premium removes the daily swipe limit and unlocks Best Shot AI.

Does SwipeCalm track what I do?

No advertising or product analytics SDK is included. RevenueCat receives purchase-related information solely to provide and restore subscriptions.
